# Accessible data visualization

- Provide a concise text summary of the key point and relevant uncertainty.
- Preserve an accessible table or structured data view when it supports the task and policy permits access.
- Give charts names, descriptions, units, time windows, and filter context.
- Keep interactive chart controls keyboard-operable with visible focus and announced state.
- Use labels, patterns, shapes, position, or icons in addition to color.
- Ensure tooltips are reachable without pointer hover and do not obscure focused content.
- Provide long descriptions for complex diagrams where a summary is insufficient.
- Test zoom, reflow, high contrast, forced colors, dark themes, and reduced motion.

Automated contrast or DOM checks do not prove that the visualization communicates its analytical meaning.
