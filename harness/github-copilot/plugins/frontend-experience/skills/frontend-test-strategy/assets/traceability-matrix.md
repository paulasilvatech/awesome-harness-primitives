# QA traceability matrix

| Trace ID | Story | Acceptance | Scenario | Risk | Layer | Environment | Result | Evidence | Owner/retest |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Results: `pass`, `fail`, `manual`, `not-applicable`, or `blocked`.

Record first-attempt status and retries separately. A manual result includes the procedure, environment, expected result, actual result, and reviewer. A blocked result names the missing environment or decision and owner.
