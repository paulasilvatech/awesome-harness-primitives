# Licensing

This repository contains material from multiple authors and upstream projects. **No single repository-wide license applies to every file.**

- A plugin or bundled component's own `LICENSE`, `LICENSE.txt`, manifest `license` field, notices, and provenance metadata govern that package.
- Third-party material remains under its original license and attribution requirements.
- Files without an explicit license notice are not automatically granted an open-source license by the repository being public.

See [docs/LICENSING.md](docs/LICENSING.md) for how to determine the license and provenance of a specific primitive or plugin.
